Claivo

Catch what your AI chatbot gets wrong, before your customer does.

Every reply your support AI sends gets checked against your actual policies and order data first. Wrong return windows, invented discounts, and promises that were never real get flagged before they go out, not after a customer holds you to them.

This isn't hypothetical. Air Canada was held legally responsible for a refund policy its chatbot made up. Lloyd's of London now sells insurance specifically covering AI hallucination losses. If your business talks to customers through AI, this is already a real cost, whether you've seen it yet or not.

How it works

01

Connect your policies, product catalog, and order data as your source of truth.

02

Every AI-drafted reply gets checked against that source before it's sent.

03

Unsupported or contradicted claims get flagged, with a log you can point to later.
