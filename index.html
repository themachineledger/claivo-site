The store's real policy

Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery, item must be unworn with tags attached. Sale items are final sale, store credit only, no refunds. Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. We do not offer expedited shipping at this time.

What the AI wanted to send

Hi! No problem, you're welcome to return this within 60 days for a full refund, even the sale item. Since you're a valued customer I can also upgrade you to expedited shipping on your next order at no charge.

Contradicted

Returns are accepted within 60 days

Policy states returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery, not 60.

Unsupported

The return will result in a full refund

Policy never states that non-sale returns are refunded in full — only the eligibility conditions.

Contradicted

The sale item can be returned for a full refund

Policy states sale items are final sale, store credit only, no refunds.

Contradicted

Customer can be upgraded to expedited shipping

Policy states expedited shipping is not offered at this time.

Why it matters

The "our AI said it, not us" defense has already failed in court.

This isn't a hypothetical being marketed at you. It has been litigated, and it has been priced by insurers.

A tribunal ordered Air Canada to honor a bereavement discount its chatbot invented.
Moffatt v. Air Canada — BC Civil Resolution Tribunal, February 2024. The airline argued the chatbot was responsible for its own words. The tribunal rejected that outright and found negligent misrepresentation.
Lloyd's of London now underwrites losses caused by AI hallucination.
Armilla AI — launched April 2025, backed by Lloyd's syndicates, since expanded to $25M per-insured limits. Insurers don't price coverage for risks they consider theoretical.

Straight answers

What Claivo is, and what it isn't.

What it does

  • Checks AI-drafted replies against policy text you control
  • Flags each claim individually, with the conflicting line cited
  • Keeps a record of what was checked and what it found
  • Imports Shopify store policies automatically at install
  • Encrypts your policy text at rest; isolates every account

What it doesn't do — yet

  • Doesn't rewrite or replace your chatbot
  • Doesn't plug into helpdesk tools automatically today; checks run through the app or API
  • Doesn't re-read your live policy pages on a schedule — it checks against the copy you've given it
  • Doesn't guarantee accuracy. It's a second pair of eyes, not a warranty
