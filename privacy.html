Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

This is a working draft, not a finished legal document. Claivo is a solo, pre-revenue project and this policy has not yet been reviewed by an attorney. It describes honestly what is and isn't implemented today, but it shouldn't be treated as final or binding until it has had professional review.

1. What we collect

  • Account information — email and business name — when you sign up or join the waitlist.
  • Source-of-truth documents you provide (policies, product catalogs, order data) so we can run verification checks against them.
  • Draft AI responses you submit for checking.
  • Basic usage data (number of checks run, timestamps) for billing and product improvement.

2. How we use it

  • To run the verification service you're using.
  • To improve the accuracy of the verification engine in aggregate — not by using your specific business data to train models shared with other customers, unless you opt in.
  • To bill you and communicate about your account.

3. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your source-of-truth documents or draft responses with other customers. Every account's data is isolated and reachable only with that account's own private access token.
  • We do not use your business data to train shared AI models.

4. Third parties

Verification is performed using Anthropic's Claude API. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms your data is not used to train Anthropic's models, and standard API request logs are retained by Anthropic for 7 days for abuse monitoring — that's Anthropic's policy, not ours; see their privacy policy for specifics. Payment processing, once implemented, will go through Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy. If you install Claivo via the Shopify App Store, Shopify is also a party to how your shop data reaches us — see section 8.

5. Data retention

Your saved source-of-truth text and account record are kept as long as your account is active. Draft responses submitted for a check are used only to produce that check's result and are not separately retained afterward. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

6. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or request deletion by contacting us. If you installed Claivo through the Shopify App Store, Shopify may route data and deletion requests to us automatically on your behalf — you don't need to do anything extra in that case.

7. Security — what's actually in place

Encryption at restYour source-of-truth text is encrypted before it is written to disk, using a server-side key. It's decrypted in memory only to serve your own authenticated request.
Encryption in transitAll traffic to our API and web app is served over HTTPS/TLS.
Account isolationEvery request that reads or writes your data must present your own private bearer token. No endpoint returns another account's data.
No customer data in logs or alertsOur internal error and monitoring alerts contain only account emails and error messages — never your source-of-truth content or draft text.
Restricted cross-origin accessOur API only accepts browser requests from our own official app domain, not arbitrary third-party sites.
Abuse preventionSignups are rate-limited per network to deter mass fake-account creation.

Not yet implemented, and we'd rather say so than imply otherwise: formal encryption key rotation, a SOC 2 or equivalent third-party audit, and a bug bounty program.

8. Shopify installations

If you install Claivo from the Shopify App Store we request only the minimum access needed to read your shop's published policies (refund, shipping, privacy, terms) so they can serve as your source of truth. We do not request or store your customers' personal information, orders, or payment details. We honor Shopify's mandatory data-protection requirements: if a customer requests their data or deletion through you and you forward it to Shopify, Shopify notifies us automatically and we respond within the required window. If you uninstall the app we delete all data associated with your shop within 48 hours.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated to active customers.

10. Contact

Reach us via the contact details on our homepage.
