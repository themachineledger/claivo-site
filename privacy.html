Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026
This is a starting draft, not a finished legal document. Claivo is a solo, pre-revenue project. This policy has not yet been reviewed by an attorney — it describes, honestly, what is and isn't implemented today, but it should not be treated as a final, legally binding document until it has had professional review.

1. What We Collect

  • Account information (email, business name) when you sign up or join the waitlist.
  • Source-of-truth documents you upload (policies, product catalogs, order data) for the purpose of running verification checks.
  • Draft AI responses submitted for checking.
  • Basic usage data (number of checks run, timestamps) for billing and product improvement.

2. How We Use It

  • To run the verification service you're using.
  • To improve the accuracy of the verification engine (in aggregate, not by using your specific business data to train models shared with other customers, unless you opt in).
  • To bill you and communicate about your account.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your source-of-truth documents or draft responses with other customers. Every account's data is isolated and accessible only via that account's own private access token.
  • We do not use your business data to train shared AI models.

4. Third Parties

Verification is performed using Anthropic's Claude API. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, your data is not used to train Anthropic's models, and standard API request logs are retained by Anthropic for 7 days for abuse-monitoring purposes (this is Anthropic's policy, not ours — see Anthropic's own privacy policy for specifics). Payment processing (once implemented) will go through Stripe, subject to Stripe's privacy policy. If you install Claivo via the Shopify App Store, Shopify is also a party to how your shop data reaches us — see Section 8.

5. Data Retention

Your saved source-of-truth text and account record are kept for as long as your account is active. Draft responses submitted for a check are used only to produce that check's result and are not separately retained afterward. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time (see Section 6).

6. Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data or request deletion by contacting us. If you installed Claivo through the Shopify App Store, Shopify may also route data requests and deletion requests to us automatically on your behalf (see Section 8) — you don't need to do anything extra in that case.

7. Security

Concrete measures actually in place today:

  • Encryption at rest: your source-of-truth text is encrypted before it is ever written to disk, using a server-side encryption key. It is only decrypted in memory, transiently, to serve your own authenticated request.
  • Encryption in transit: all traffic to our API and web app is served over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Account isolation via access tokens: every request that reads or writes your data must present your own private bearer token; there is no endpoint that returns another account's data.
  • No data in logs or alerts: our internal error/monitoring alerts include only account emails and error messages — never your source-of-truth content or draft text.
  • Restricted cross-origin access: our API only accepts browser requests from our own official app domain, not from arbitrary third-party websites.
  • Basic abuse prevention: signups are rate-limited per network to deter mass fake-account creation.

Still on the roadmap, not yet implemented: formal encryption key rotation, a SOC 2 or similar third-party security audit, and a bug bounty program. We'll be upfront about this list shrinking over time rather than claiming a completed audit that hasn't happened.

8. Shopify Installations

If you install Claivo from the Shopify App Store, we request only the minimum access needed to read your shop's published policies (refund, shipping, privacy, terms of service) so we can automatically use them as your source-of-truth — we do not request or store your customers' personal information, orders, or payment details. We honor Shopify's mandatory data-protection requirements: if a customer requests their data or requests deletion through you, and you forward that request to Shopify, Shopify notifies us automatically and we respond within the required window. If you uninstall the app, we delete all data associated with your shop within 48 hours.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be communicated to active customers.

10. Contact

Reach us via the contact details on our homepage.
